A crew with a job to do.

The Resolute is a damaged exploration ship. Your 16 crew members make local decisions, follow a captain-led hierarchy, and physically travel to carry out their orders.

  1. Set the captain's priority. Explore, repair, research or look after the crew. Department heads create and delegate the work.
  2. Watch the ship. Engineers diagnose, fabricate and repair. Medical treats injuries. Security contains incidents and checks crew safety. Operations handles supplies, salvage and navigation.
  3. Go beyond the ship. Choose an adjacent destination on the sector map, or enable autonomous navigation. Scientists survey each new sector for data, fuel and materials.
  4. Build a better vessel. Research unlocks repair drones, adaptive shields and hydroponics. Engineers can construct them once materials are available.
  5. Complete the expedition. Map every compartment, survey five sectors, and develop three technologies. Continue exploring after the objective is complete.

These are simulated decision-making agents. No language model, API key or internet connection is needed. Time advances while this page is active. Autosave stays on this device; use Save voyage to keep a portable backup.

Keyboard: Space pauses, 1 / 2 / 5 changes speed, M switches views. Room and destination buttons provide keyboard access to the map.
